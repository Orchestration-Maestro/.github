# Support

The organization's default support guide. A repository with a `SUPPORT.md` of
its own shows that one instead.

## Where your problem goes

| You have | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Something that does not work as documented | The repository's **Bug report** form |
| An improvement, or a question the documentation does not answer | The repository's **Feature request** form, saying what you looked for |
| A suspected vulnerability or an exposed credential | The [security policy](https://github.com/Orchestration-Maestro/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md), privately, never in a public issue |

No support address, team or response time is promised.

## What to include

- The exact version, tag or commit.
- The commands you ran, what you expected and what happened.
- The output, with tokens, credentials and private paths removed.
